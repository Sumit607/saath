Saath

Your friends' phones become your speaker.

Start a party on your Android phone. Friends scan the QR code and their phones and laptops play your music in sync. They install nothing.

Download for Android Latest version, Android 10 or later

Install it

  1. Tap Download for Android, then open the file.
  2. If Android asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps.
  3. Tap Install. If Play Protect warns about an unknown developer, tap Install anyway.
  4. After that, Saath updates itself. The first time, allow Saath to install updates when it asks.
ONLINE

Same Wi-Fi

At home or in the hostel, everyone joins the same Wi-Fi and scans one QR code.

OFFLINE

No internet at all

Outdoors, your phone makes its own Wi-Fi. Friends scan two QR codes: one for the Wi-Fi, one for the party.

MY MUSIC

Songs on your phone

Pick songs from your phone's music library, or the built-in test beat.

ANY APP

Whatever you play

Share the sound from Spotify, YouTube Music and other apps that allow it.

In sync, measured

Every phone and laptop has its own hidden audio delay. Tap Tune up: each device plays a short chirp, your phone's microphone hears exactly when each one arrives, and Saath corrects each device. Then it checks again and shows the gap between speakers in milliseconds.
